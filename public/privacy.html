Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Workout Program is a personal training app built for, and used by, a single person (its owner). Sign-in is limited to the owner's own Google account.

What the app uses

  • Google sign-in, to confirm the owner is the one using the app.
  • Google Sheets / Drive, to back up workout logs to a spreadsheet in the owner's own Drive.
  • Google Calendar, to read the owner's "BJJ Trained" events and to add finished workouts to the owner's "Workouts" calendar.
  • Google Health, to write the owner's training sessions and active calories to the owner's own Google Health account.

What happens to the data

Data stays in the owner's own Google account and in the app's own storage. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No one other than the owner has access to it.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security → Third-party apps with account access.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
